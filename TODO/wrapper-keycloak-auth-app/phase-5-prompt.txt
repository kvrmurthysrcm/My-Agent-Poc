Goal:

Protected wrapper endpoints call downstream services using X-API-Key.
JWT is not forwarded.

Deliverables:

app/services/downstream_client.py
app/routes/rag_gateway_routes.py
tests/test_rag_gateway_routes.py

Success criteria:

/rag/search calls http://localhost:8001/rag/search
/rag/ingest calls http://localhost:8000/rag/ingest
/rag/ask calls http://localhost:8002/rag/ask
Downstream receives X-API-Key.
Downstream does not receive Authorization header.

- Requires role rag_user OR rag_admin.
